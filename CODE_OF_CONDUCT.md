# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Limes Axis contributors and maintainers commit to a professional, respectful and
inclusive project environment. Participation should improve the software and the
community without compromising anyone's safety, privacy or dignity.

## Expected Behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Give and receive technical feedback with clarity and good faith.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels and communication styles.
- Protect private, personal, customer and security-sensitive information.
- Accept maintainer decisions on scope, safety and moderation once the rationale
  has been explained.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or sexualized attention.
- Personal insults, sustained disruption or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing credentials, exploit details, customer material or proprietary logs in
  public project spaces.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and
other project spaces, and when someone represents Limes Axis in public.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report conduct incidents privately to a repository maintainer through the GitHub
organization or repository contact surface. For vulnerabilities, follow
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md). Do not open a public issue containing sensitive
evidence.

Maintainers will limit access to reports, request only the information needed to
assess them and avoid conflicts of interest where practical. Responses may
include a private clarification, warning, content removal, temporary restriction
or permanent exclusion, proportionate to the conduct and risk. Knowingly false
or retaliatory reports are themselves violations of this policy.
